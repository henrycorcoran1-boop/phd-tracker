AWDS Management Team — Roles & ResponsibilitiesCore responsibilities by role, with the wider team beneath each lead
AWDS Project Director
  • Overall project leadership, strategy and key decision-making
  • Ultimate accountability for safety, quality, programme & budget
  • Senior client / TII relationship, governance & major change
AWDS Project Management Lead
  • Day-to-day leadership of delivery across all workstreams & packages
  • Owns programme, resourcing, risk, change and reporting
  • Single point of accountability; leads team integration
Reporting to the AWDS Project Management Lead
AWDS Deputy Project Manager
  • Deputises for the PM Lead; supports daily delivery
  • Coordinates operations, interfaces & milestones
  • Drives consistent process and progress tracking
Team: Project support, administration & office management
AWDS Design Management Lead
  • Leads & integrates multidisciplinary design
  • Owns design programme, interfaces & technical integration
  • Manages the design reachback team and design change
Team: Design discipline leads (reachback), BIM & GIS, Information Management
AWDS PMO Lead
  • Establishes PMO governance, systems & controls
  • Owns reporting, KPIs, baseline & information mgmt
  • Coordinates finance, document control & assurance
Team: Project controls, planning/scheduling, document control, risk
AWDS Technical Delivery Lead
  • Ensures technical compliance & verification
  • Manages assurance processes & technical documentation
  • Manages the design-to-construction technical interface
Team: Contracts / technical engineers, QA/QC, CEMP / environmental
AWDS Procurement Lead
  • Leads procurement strategy & supply-chain engagement
  • Manages tendering, evaluation and contract award
  • Ensures procurement compliance and governance
Team: Procurement package leads & procurement support
AWDS Commercial Lead
  • Owns commercial strategy, cost control & change management
  • Reviews scope documents for commercial ambiguity & consistency with the commercial / procurement strategy
  • Owns the interface with contract development for each M100 contract
  • Owns the interface with Pinsent Masons to develop agreements
  • Manages NEC contract administration & commercial risk
  • Leads commercial reporting, estimating & value management
Team: Quantity surveyors, estimators, cost management, commercial project controls
AWDS Cost Management Leadreports to the Commercial Lead
  • Manages cost planning, estimating & forecasting
  • Controls budgets, cash flow and cost reporting
  • Supports change valuation & commercial assurance
Team: Cost estimators & cost clerks
AWDS Contract Manager(typical of all Contract Managers)
  • Acts as the Project Manager for their contract / package
  • Owns package delivery — safety, quality, programme & budget
  • Construction stage: full-time point of contact for RFIs, designs, site meetings & reporting
Team: Contracts engineer, as-built / doc control, QS, utilities liaison, CEMP

Contract Management — Meeting Cadence & Monthly RhythmHow each contract is managed every month
Package-lead ownership: Contract & Procurement Package Leads own their package meetings, action logs and monthly reporting — keeping the wider team joined up. Standard agendas and a common monthly report format are used across all contracts.
