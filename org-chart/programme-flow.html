AWDS Design-to-Tender WorkflowThe stages each M1XX contract package passes through
STAGE 2 — DESIGN DEVELOPMENT → TENDER
1
Pre-IDC Pack Issued
Design pack prepared and issued ready for the inter-disciplinary check.
2
IDC
Inter-Disciplinary Check — multidiscipline design review & coordination.
3
Full ITT Pack Issued — Rev 01
Complete tender design pack issued at Rev 01 for the package.
4
TII Instruction to Issue C01
TII instructs AWDS to issue the C01 (construction-issue) drawings.
5
AWDS C01 Submission
AWDS submits the C01 drawings to TII (C01 Drawings Submission to TII).
6
Ready for Tender
Tender documentation complete, checked and assured.
7
ITT Issue Date
Invitation to Tender issued to market — design-driven.
each package then flows into procurement & award
STAGE 3 — PROCUREMENT & AWARD
8
Tender Period
Contractors price the works; queries & clarifications managed.
9
ITT Award
Preferred tenderer selected and the ITT awarded.
10
Contract Award
NEC contract executed with the appointed contractor.
11
Construction Start
Works commence on site; AWDS moves to construction-stage support.
Each M1XX contract package follows the same workflow through these stages.
