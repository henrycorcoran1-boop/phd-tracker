The programme on one page. Read down a column for a workstream and across the lower bands for what it produces. Six dependencies cross a lane boundary and are drawn as dashed connectors: the item pool from A5 into B5, the mechanism analysis from B4 into C4, the frozen instrument from B7 into C7, the pilot parameters from B7 and the calibrated parameters from C6 into D1, and the artefact from D4 into E2. Everything else runs inside its own lane, which is why the five workstreams proceed concurrently.

One structural decision for the supervisor

The seven-chapter structure carried from the thesis draft places artefact design, artefact evaluation and the theoretical discussion together in Chapter 6. That chapter will be substantially longer than the others. Two options exist and the choice belongs with the supervisor rather than in this document: keep seven chapters and accept an uneven Chapter 6, or split it into an artefact chapter and a discussion chapter for eight. Nothing else in this roadmap changes either way; only the chapter column of the table above does.
