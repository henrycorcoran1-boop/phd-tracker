Appendix F

Binding ThresholdsEvery analytical cutoff in one place, with the authority behind each

Guardrail G6 requires that every threshold live in one version-controlled file with its source attached, and that the code read it from there. This appendix is the human-readable form of config/thresholds.yml. Where the two disagree, one of them is wrong and is corrected; they are never left to disagree. Author-year references resolve in Appendix G, and each must be confirmed against the publisher record before it is carried into a manuscript.

On the word threshold

Several of the values above are conventions rather than laws, and two of the most-used were derived under conditions that do not match this design. They are adopted because a stated convention with its source attached is defensible and an unstated one is not. Where a value is used, it is cited; where it is contested, the contest is acknowledged in the methods chapter rather than concealed by silence. That is the whole of the argument for having this appendix.
